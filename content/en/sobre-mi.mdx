I am a full-stack developer working from Morelia. What I do is almost always the
same, whatever the industry: someone runs a process on spreadsheets and
patience, and I turn it into a system that gets used every day.

Monday to Friday I am at **URVENUE**, in the Web Services department, where I
manage and implement the integrations clients use to sell and administer their
tickets, admissions and packages. Outside of that I have shipped production
products to three clients of my own, and I build my own tools to measure and
produce the content I publish.

## How I deliver

When I close a project I also deliver **its decisions in writing**: why each
thing was chosen, what was discarded and what was left pending. That is what
lets someone else — or me, six months later — pick it up without rebuilding the
reasoning from scratch.

Every project in my documentation also carries its list of known debts: what was
left half-done and what has to be solved before growing. A problem written down
stops being a surprise.
